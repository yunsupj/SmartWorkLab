# Privacy Policy for SmartWorkLab

**Last Updated: January 30, 2026**

SmartWorkLab ("we," "our," or "us"), operated by yuunchloe, is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our website [smartworklab.store](https://smartworklab.store) (the "Service").

By accessing or using our Service, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Personal Information
When you register for an account using **OAuth (Google, Discord)**, we collect email addresses and public profile information (name, avatar) solely to create your account and provide personalized ROI analysis. We do not store your password.

### B. Usage Data
We collect information on how you interact with our Service, including:
- Tools you add to your dashboard
- Usage metrics (costs, hours saved) you input
- Pages visited and interaction times

### C. Cookies and Tracking Technologies
We use cookies to maintain your session and improve user experience.
- **Session Cookies**: Managed via `@supabase/ssr` to keep you logged in.
- **AdSense & DoubleClick DART Cookies**:
    - Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on our Service.
    - Google's use of the DART cookie enables it to serve ads to users based on their visit to our Service and other sites on the Internet.
    - Users may opt-out of the use of the DART cookie by visiting the [Google Ad and Content Network Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data for the following purposes:
- **Service Provision**: To generate personalized ROI reports, "Expert Lab" reviews, and efficiency insights.
- **Optimization**: To analyze user behavior using tools like our "Scout Agent" to improve our algorithm's accuracy.
- **Communication**: To send important updates regarding your account or service changes.

## 3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is securely stored using **Supabase**, a trusted backend-as-a-service provider. We implement industry-standard security measures, including encryption and strict access controls (Row Level Security), to protect your personal information.

## 4. Third-Party Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your Personally Identifiable Information to outside parties, except for trusted third parties who assist us in operating our website (e.g., Vercel, Supabase), so long as those parties agree to keep this information confidential.

Our site may contain links to third-party AI tools (e.g., affiliate links). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those external sites.

## 5. User Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:
- **Access**: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
- **Correction**: Request correction of inaccurate data.
- **Deletion**: Request deletion of your account and associated data.
- **Opt-Out**: You can opt-out of marketing communications at any time.

To exercise these rights, please contact us at **support@smartworklab.store** (or contact Yunsup Jung directly).

## 6. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
